ABOUT A.R.RAHMAN

A.R. Rahman is an internationally acclaimed Indian composer, singer, and music producer, celebrated for his innovative sound and timeless melodies. Known as the “Mozart of Madras,” he has transformed Indian film music by blending traditional Indian sounds with global musical influences.

With two Academy Awards, two Grammy Awards, and numerous prestigious honors, Rahman continues to inspire millions through his music and his remarkable contribution to the world of cinema.
